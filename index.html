Dr. Ankita Sharma has over eight years of experience in advanced dental care, with a post-graduate specialization in Prosthodontics and Implantology from one of India's leading institutions. Her approach combines clinical precision with a strong focus on aesthetics to deliver natural, confident smiles.

At Prime Dental Clinic, every patient receives personalized attention and thoughtful care.
